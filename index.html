The game

A farming game where everything has physics

Every bale, cob, pumpkin, pig and farmer is a real physics object. There's no quota and no game over — just a shared wallet, a long list of upgrades, and a crew that keeps getting in each other's way.

Harvest

Square bales for one, round bales for two. Cut corn, dig potatoes, grow giant pumpkins.

Stack & haul

Pile it high on the flatbed, hop on the tractor and pray at every bump.

Sell

Drop it in the Market Stall pen. Rescued loot pays double; flood the market and prices sag.

Upgrade & fight

Spend the kitty in the catalogue, then ring the dinner bell for the Raccoon King.

Physics chaos

One very wobbly trailer

Your whole day's work rides on a hitched flatbed that bounces, sways and tips. Drive like it.

  • Lift round bales together, throw square bales for bullseye trick shots.
  • Ride on the load while a friend drives — and fly off it at the first bump.
  • Get flattened by a runaway bale and ragdoll across the yard. Your crew can carry you… or throw you onto the trailer.
  • Tractors, combines, a potato harvester and the Mega Trailer, each with engines that wear, leak oil and break down.
Thieves

Hay bandits & the Raccoon King

The wildlife wants your harvest more than you do.

  • Raccoons sneak out of their burrows and team up to carry off round bales. Shove them, bonk them, or run them over.
  • Kernel Crows dive for the cobs in your arms. Gus the Goose steals the tractor's key.
  • Ring the dinner bell and the Raccoon King rolls up in a shopping cart, ropes your trailer and tows it home. Pelt him with bales, then tackle him off his throne.
  • Fight back with the Egg Slingshot, a Scarecrow, Zapper Gates and a squad of Guard Geese.
The crew

Better with friends. Worse for the farm.

Host a farm and share a join code. Solo works too — it's the same game, just quieter.

  • End-of-day awards like Best Navigator, Gatekeeper and Spud Stud.
  • Emote wheel, pings, feats and a porch wardrobe full of daft outfits.
  • Whack sleeping crewmates out of bed with the Rubber Chicken.
  • Weeks, weekly goals and a Farm Level that keeps going as long as you do.
The job board

Every morning, a new job

Flip the job board on the porch to pick tomorrow's work. Each one plays differently, and each one goes wrong differently.

Hay

Bale Out!

Square and round bales across the fields. Stack the trailer, mind the bump.

Corn

Maize Craze

Drive the combine through 2.5 m corn. Catch the cobs, swat the crows, kick the jammed engine.

Pigs

Hog Wild

Six greased pigs loose on the farm. Two to carry one. The slop bucket helps. The mud doesn't.

Potatoes

Spud Rush

Hitch the Potato Harvester, dig the rows, then tip a whole avalanche of spuds into the sell pen.

The fairground

Show off for spare change

Skill and chaos games next to the market. No gambling — just bragging rights and a few extra Bucks.

  • Greased Pig — let it loose, chase it round the pen, get it in the sty before the clock runs out.
  • Hay Bale Bowling — ten pins, one bale, no farmers on the lane.
  • Bale Tower — stack twelve bales as high as you dare and hold it for five seconds.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What kind of game is Farmers?

A co-op physics comedy about running a farm with your friends. You harvest crops, stack them on a wobbly trailer, sell them at the market, buy upgrades and defend your harvest from raccoons, crows and one very determined goose.

How many players?

One to four. One player hosts the farm and friends join with a short code. Steam friend invites are on the way. Solo is the same game with nobody else joining — no sign-in and no internet needed.

Can I play it alone?

Yes. Some things are easier with friends (round bales and pigs take two to carry) but there are upgrades like the Bale Spike and the Lasso that let a lone farmer manage.

Is there a game over or a time limit?

No. There's no quota and you can't lose the farm. Days pass, weeks bring goals and a Farm Level, and the Raccoon King waits until you ring the bell.

Which platforms?

Windows PC, on Steam. A demo is planned before release.

When does it come out?
